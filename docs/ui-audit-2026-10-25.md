# UI audit, 2026-10-25

Why: the owner found the profile pill turning fully white, uneven gaps in the top bar, a Play button
in the old style beside a trophy "pill", missing hover states and an odd clock. This audit lists every
interactive element type of the launcher as it was on `main` at 760ccfd (before the system in
`docs/DESIGN.md` 3.9), and where the types disagree. Sources: `ui/theme.rs`, `ui/components.rs`,
`ui/chrome.rs`, `screens/home.rs`, `screens/settings.rs`, `screens/trophies.rs`, `screens/online/feed.rs`,
`screens/whatsnew.rs`, `screens/library.rs`, `screens/onboarding.rs`.

## Where they disagree

1. **Focus is a white blob.** Every focus stop except the tiles and the trophy row fills solid white and
   swaps its ink to black, and most also grow 3 dp. On the profile pill that is a white capsule that
   reads as a primary action, not as "the cursor is here"; the growth reserves layout room, so the
   whole strip shifts when focus moves. The tiles, the one element the owner likes, use a ring.
2. **Two focus languages.** Tiles and trophy rows use a ring; all else uses a white fill. Ring width
   is 3 on tiles, 2 on trophy rows.
3. **No hover on the tiles and library cells**, the reference the owner named. Chevrons, switches, row
   buttons and cards each invent their own hover (disc, 0.24, 0.18, 0.14); none eases (hover is a hard
   cut, only focus has a spring).
4. **The action row has three families.** Play and `•••` are 54 high rounded rectangles (r 14); the
   trophy button is a 32 high capsule. The top bar is 44 high capsules and circles. Heights 54 / 44 /
   32, radii 14 / 22 / capsule, with no rule for which a control uses.
5. **Play is gray.** The prominent variant is white 0.26, which is the hover colour of everything else:
   it neither reads as the primary action nor as a button at rest.
6. **Top-bar gaps.** Library, Friends, Settings sit 12 apart; the profile pill sits 32 from Settings
   (`group_gap - button_gap`), and the clock 32 from the pill.
7. **Clock.** 20 medium at white 0.92, vertically placed by the line box (not the cap height), so its
   digits sit low against the 44 high buttons; no label of its own; it reads as a stray label.
8. **Shadows behind translucent fills.** Console and icon buttons paint a white glow shadow under a
   translucent fill, which draws a ghost seam inside the shape.
9. **Pressed** exists only on buttons, chips and pills (0.97); rows, cards, cells and tiles have none.
10. **Disabled** is 40 % on buttons and switches, 62 % ink on rows; no tooltip or reason anywhere.
11. **No visible focus indicator contrast rule.** White-on-white focus has no contrast against its
    own pale surroundings (a focused row on a lit panel), and the ring is only on tiles.

## What to do about it

One family and one focus language, defined in `theme.rs` and drawn by `components.rs`; see
DESIGN.md 3.9. Rows, cards and cells move to the same focus treatment so that "where am I" always looks
the same. Hover for every pointer-reachable control. The profile screen agent consumes the same
components.

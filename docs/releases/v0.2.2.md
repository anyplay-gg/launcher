## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.2

### How did it run?

- After you play a game while signed in, the game's page asks once, quietly: **How did it run?** Choose Doesn't start, Menus, In game or Playable, or close the question. It asks once per game version and toolkit version, and never while you are signed out.
- Only the game's title ID and version, the toolkit version, your answer and your system (Windows, Linux or macOS) are sent. Nothing about your PC or your games' files.
- Once at least three players have answered, the game's facts show **Runs**, for example "Playable · 12 reports". The same answers make up the public list at [anyplaynet.com/compatibility](https://anyplaynet.com/compatibility).

### The game page

- When friends play the game you have selected, a line under Play says so: "Mira is playing this".
- The trophy count sits as a small chip right next to Play and opens the game's trophies.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.2-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.2-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.2-x86_64.AppImage` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`. The Linux build does not update itself yet.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself; it is signed like every update since 0.2.0.

## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.7

### New

- **Captures**: a full-screen gallery of your screenshots and clips, newest first and grouped by game. Open it from the top bar or from the Home button panel during a game. Filter by screenshots or clips, look at a screenshot full screen, open a clip in your video player, copy, show in folder or delete (it asks first).
- **The PS button works while a game runs.** On a DualSense or DualShock 4, over USB or Bluetooth, the PS button now brings up the launcher like the Guide button does. On Linux this needs read access to the controller; [docs/INSTALL.md](https://github.com/anyplaynet/launcher/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) has the rule to add.
- **Play Together knows each game's version rule.** Some games only play together on the exact same version, others on the same major version. When your version differs from your friend's, **Try anyway** joins regardless; it may not connect or may fall out of sync.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): the first Mac build of the launcher. Online features and automatic updates on the Mac come in a later version.

### Look and feel

- The game before the selected one stays visible, dimmed, at the left of the home row, so you always see where you came from. Thanks to lenny4250 for this one.
- Compare and Profile have a Back button for the mouse.
- Text, surfaces and corners use fewer, steadier values across the launcher. This is the first step of a polish pass that continues in the next version.

### Changes

- **Create Account** now shows links to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy before you sign up.
- **Show Game on Discord** is off for new profiles. Turn it on in **Settings > Behavior** if you want it. A choice you already made is kept.
- The mouse's back button works as Back.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.7-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.7-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.7-x86_64.AppImage` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.7-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`.
- macOS: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.7-macos-arm64.zip`. Unzip it and move the app to Applications. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have on Windows or Linux offers this update by itself. It is signed, like every update since 0.2.0.

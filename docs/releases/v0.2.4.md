## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.4

### Your Profile

- Your profile button now opens a full-screen profile: your games, hours and trophies, recent trophies and recent activity. Friends' profiles look the same and open from the friends list, a friend's page or a feed card.
- A friend's profile shows only what they share. Games you own show their name and art; Back returns you to where you were.
- **Edit Profile** opens your account on the web, and **Friends & Online** opens the online panel.

### Look and Feel

- Buttons, pills and icon buttons now share one shape and three sizes, so Play, the trophy button and `...` match.
- Focus is a ring around buttons, rows and cards instead of a white fill. Nothing covers its neighbours or moves when focus moves.
- Every control reacts when the mouse is over it, and so do game tiles.
- Library, Friends, Settings and your profile sit evenly spaced in the top bar. The clock is quieter and stays still when the minute changes.
- The round top buttons no longer show thin lines inside.

### First Start

- The welcome now asks whether you want to play together: **Sign In**, **Create Account** or **Not Now**. You can still sign in later from Online.

### Phone as Controller

- Use your phone as a gamepad, and browse your library on it, start games and see trophies as they pop. It works with the AnyPS5 Remote phone app.
- Turn it on in **Settings > Phone as Controller**, then pair the phone with the four-digit code shown on both screens. Paired phones are listed there and can be removed.
- Only phones on your home network can connect, one at a time. It is off until you turn it on.
- On Windows the phone plays through the ViGEmBus driver: the settings page says whether it is installed and links the official download. The launcher never installs it for you. On Linux it needs access to `/dev/uinput`; the page shows the rule to add.

### Calls

- A call uses Voice Activity while it lasts, so you no longer hold a key to talk. If you chose Push to Talk yourself, calls keep it. Parties keep your setting either way, and the strip says which mode a call uses.

### Smaller fixes

- **Settings > About** links the AnyPlay website, the Discord, the privacy policy and the terms, and says what the launcher is built on.
- What's New is easier to read, with each area under its own title.
- The library fits as many columns as the window has room for.
- If unlocking a game stops, the launcher says what happened and what to do next.
- The Discord link now points at an invite that does not expire.
- A reaction you just gave in the feed stays put while the feed refreshes.
- Linked Steam accounts show the Steam logo on profiles.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.4-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.4-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.4-x86_64.AppImage` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.4-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`. The Linux build does not update itself yet.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself; it is signed like every update since 0.2.0.

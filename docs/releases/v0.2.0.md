## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.0

The launcher is now called **AnyPlay Launcher**. It has an online area with friends, parties and party voice, and from this release on its updates are signed.

### New name

AnyPS5 Launcher is now AnyPlay Launcher. AnyPS5 is still the engine your games run on. Installing the new Setup over an old install upgrades it in place and keeps your settings, library and saves. Your data folders and `anyps5://` links keep their old names on purpose, so nothing breaks.

### Online play and friends

- The top of the home screen shows your account, or **Sign In** when you are signed out. **Sign In** and **Create Account** open the AnyPlay cloud at `https://anyplaynet.com` in your browser, where you use a passkey or an e-mail code. The launcher takes you in when you are done.
- The new **Friends** button next to Library and Settings shows how many friends are online and opens your friends list. Friend requests wait at the top with Accept and Decline, and each friend shows what they play.
- Send and accept friend requests and game invitations, and block or report someone.
- Start a **party**, invite friends to it and send messages.
- Trophies are recorded with your account. A friend's page shows how many trophies they have, by grade. Names and pictures stay on your PC, and **Who Sees Your Trophies** on the Account page decides who sees them.
- Your playtime per game and day is recorded with your profile.
- **Hide Your IP Address** sends all traffic through a relay, so other players never learn your address. Games may respond a little slower.
- New builds use the AnyPlay cloud at `https://anyplaynet.com` by default. It is new, so tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/nSHbHBRdS) when something breaks. You can enter another cloud under **Online, Cloud**.
- No real game plays online yet. Games start offline until your AnyPS5 toolkit carries the network bridge.

### Party voice

- The party page has a **Voice** group: switch party voice on or off, see whether your microphone is muted, open or talking, and choose **Push to Talk** or **Voice Activity**. Members who speak are marked "Speaking".
- Open any member of the party to set their volume (0 to 200 percent) or mute them for yourself.
- **Voice Settings** holds the talk key, the microphone and the speakers. Your choices are remembered.
- Push to talk works while the launcher has the focus, because the launcher reads no keys outside its own window. To talk while a game is in front, use Voice Activity.
- If the system does not allow the microphone, or there is no microphone, the launcher says so and switches voice off.

### Account and safety

- When the AnyPlay cloud restricts an account, the launcher says what is restricted, until when (in your time zone), why, and how to appeal, instead of a generic failure. A muted account can send an appeal from the launcher. For a ban, **Appeal in Browser** opens the cloud's page.
- While friend requests or messages are paused, Add a Friend and the conversation show a calm "paused" row, and your draft waits.
- E-mail, passkeys, devices and account deletion are managed on the cloud's web page (**Manage Account** on the Account page), not inside the launcher. The launcher opens only addresses under the cloud you use.

### Updates

From now on every update is signed. The launcher installs a new version only if its signature matches the key built into the launcher, the offer has not expired, and the file is exactly the one that was signed. If anything is wrong, it skips the update and keeps what you have. Launchers up to 0.1.3 do not check a signature, so they take this release one last time the old way, and every launcher after that checks.

The update check itself is stricter too: downloads have a size limit and time limits, redirects are limited and https only, and a downloaded update is checked again right before it is installed.

Every file in this release also has a build attestation. You can check one with `gh attestation verify <file> --repo anyplaynet/launcher`, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums. The files are still not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask once.

### Fixes and smaller changes

- Each game shows how long you played it, and its save data can be backed up and restored under Game Settings, Save Data. Backups are made automatically before Unlock Again, before a game is removed and after you play.
- **Toolkit for This Game** in a game's settings keeps that game on one toolkit version when a newer one breaks it. Every other game follows your global choice, and Settings, Toolkit lists versions no game uses any more so you can remove them.
- Hidden trophies: select **Show** on a hidden trophy to see it, or turn on **Always Show Hidden Trophies** under Behavior. This stays on your PC.
- Panel headers keep clear of the window buttons, and focused buttons keep their spacing.
- Every package now carries the licences of all third-party code (`THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`).
- The release also has `AnyPS5-Launcher-0.2.0-x64.exe`, the same program as the portable exe under the old name, so copies of 0.1.2 and 0.1.3 can find their update. You do not need it.

### Downloads

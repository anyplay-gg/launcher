## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.5

### Fixes

- The launcher now keeps its own log (`Logs\launcher.log`) and records a crash there. A bug report includes the end of it.
- The Friends screen reads as one page: its title lines up with the rows, long lists fade out at the edge instead of being cut in the middle of a line, "Playing Together" waits until someone else is in it, and letter avatars grow with the picture.
- Back sounds like a step back when it leads to another screen, and like a close only when it leads home. A burst of messages chimes once.

## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.5

### New

- **AnyPlay Engine** is a new toolkit channel in Settings. It downloads the latest nightly build of the engine, so you get the newest network features without building anything. Every download is checked against its published checksum before it is installed, and the build you had stays in place until the new one is ready.
- **Clips** (Windows): while a game runs, the launcher keeps its last 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds and saves them as an MP4 next to your screenshots. Turn it on in **Settings > Behavior > Clips**, pick a key (Alt+F12 by default) or press the Create button on your controller, and use **Show Clips** to open the folder. It is off until you turn it on.
- **Home button**: while a game runs, the Home button of your controller (or the phone controller) brings the launcher in front of the game and back. It opens a quick panel with Back to Game, Trophies, Game Settings, Friends and Party, and Close Game, which asks first.
- **Friends is a full screen.** Friends, Party, Messages, Activity and your online settings sit on one page with a rail on the left, in the same family as your profile. The side drawer is gone.
- **Appear Offline**: switch it on in your account settings or from your own profile. Friends see you as offline while you keep playing.
- **Levels, points and trophy compare**: your profile shows your level and points. Open a game you share with a friend to compare trophies side by side.
- **Share Screen on Linux** now works with Cisco's OpenH264 video codec. Turn it on in **Settings > Advanced**; the launcher downloads the codec from Cisco when you do, and the page shows its state.
- **Linux updates itself.** The AppImage now updates through the same signed check as the Windows exe.
- The launcher checks for updates every few hours while it is open. A quiet notice offers **Restart Now** or **Later** and never interrupts a running game. **Settings > About** says plainly what happened if a check fails.

### Faster

- **Unlocking a game again** takes about 30 seconds instead of more than two minutes when nothing in the game changed.
- **New games are prepared in the background**, like on a console, so they are ready sooner when you want to play.
- The toolkit opens faster because it is no longer re-checked file by file at every start.
- The idle backdrop and artwork use less of your graphics card.
- Unlock checks your free disk space first and tells you if game files must be copied instead of linked.

### Look and feel

- Controller and keyboard movement feel the same: holding a direction repeats at one pace, the stick no longer flutters at the edge of its range, and a direction with nowhere to go gives a small bump.
- Your controller vibrates softly for select, back, errors, trophies, calls and the edge of a list.
- Moving the mouse onto a button, tile or row plays the navigation tick.
- New sounds for open, close, on, off, notifications, messages and invitations. Back sounds like a step back when it leads to another screen, and like a close only when it leads home.
- Pressing a control looks pressed. The old hero image leaves quickly when you move to another game, and with reduced motion on, things fade in place instead of moving.
- Text and shades were checked for contrast, and screen readers follow the focus.

### Fixes

- Unlocking again never loses your saves or trophies if the swap fails.
- Games that load a `.prx` file placed next to `eboot.bin` (Minecraft, for one) now start. Thanks to lenny4250 for the fix.
- A trophy you have earned is never shown as secret.
- On Linux, the launcher tells you when your system's glibc is older than the games need.
- The launcher keeps its own log (`Logs\launcher.log`) and records a crash there. A bug report includes the end of it.
- The Friends screen reads as one page: its title lines up with the rows, long lists fade out at the edge instead of being cut in the middle of a line, "Playing Together" waits until someone else is in it, and letter avatars grow with the picture.
- A burst of messages chimes once.

### Downloads

- The launcher you have offers this update by itself; it is signed like every update since 0.2.0.

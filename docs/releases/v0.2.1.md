## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.1

### Games get system fonts by default

- Games no longer need a Fonts folder. When you have not set one, the launcher gives your games the Noto fonts it carries, for Latin, monospace, Thai and Arabic text. Some games, such as Astro's Playroom, stopped early without system fonts and now start.
- Japanese, Chinese and Korean fonts are downloaded the first time a game needs them, from the official Noto release, and checked before they are used.
- **Settings, Fonts** says which fonts your games get. If you set your own Fonts folder, the launcher uses it as before.

### Also in this release

- Everything from [0.2.0](https://github.com/anyplaynet/launcher/releases/tag/v0.2.0): the new top bar with your account or **Sign In**, the **Friends** button and friends list, **Toolkit for This Game**, and hidden trophies you can show.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.1-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.1-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.1-x86_64.AppImage` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`. The Linux build does not update itself yet.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself; it is signed like every update since 0.2.0.

## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.3

### Calls

- Call an online friend from their page. On their side a calm card rings with your name, Accept and Decline, and a soft ring from the launcher's own sounds.
- While you talk, a small strip shows who is in the call and who is speaking, with Mute, Deafen and Leave Call. Add to Call rings another friend into the same call.
- A call you did not answer shows as "Missed call" on the friend's row until you look.
- **Voice On: AnyPlay or Discord.** On Discord the launcher never opens your microphone for a party or a call. The party leader can add the party's Discord channel link, and everyone gets Open in Discord.

### Your profile and the feed

- **Your Profile** in the online area shows what other players see: your picture, your line about you, up to three favourite games, trophies, most played and the accounts you linked. Edit Profile opens your account on the web.
- **View Profile** on a friend's page opens theirs. Parts they keep private are left out.
- **Feed:** what you and your friends did lately, newest first: trophies with how rare they are, new friends, games played for the first time, parties. Answer with Nice, GG, Wow or Same. There are no comments.

### Join from Discord

- With Show Game on Discord on, friends on Discord get a **Join on AnyPlay** button on your profile while you are in a session or a party. The link asks you, it never joins by itself: your friend's invitation waits in the online area with Join and Decline.
- Turn the button off with "Show a Join Button on Discord" in Settings.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.3-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.3-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.3-x86_64.AppImage` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.3-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`. The Linux build does not update itself yet.
- The launcher you have offers this update by itself; it is signed like every update since 0.2.0.

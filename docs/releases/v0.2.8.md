## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.8

Every page of the launcher has been checked and cleaned up. This version also adds LudusCtrl and a proper look on the Mac.

### New

- **LudusCtrl** (Settings > Phone as Controller, Windows and Linux): a controller paired to your phone plays as this PC's pad.
  - With the LudusCtrl app on Android, or with no app at all: scan the QR code the launcher shows and play in the phone's browser. This works on iPhone too, with iOS 15.2 or newer.
  - The first time you use it, Windows asks about the firewall once, and the phone's browser warns once about the launcher's own certificate.
  - LudusCtrl protocol and app by Josip Parlov (@parl0v).
- **A Mac look** on macOS:
  - The window is see-through glass with the system blur, with the Mac's window buttons, an app menu and ⌘ shortcuts.
  - It turns solid in full screen, while a game runs, and when Reduce Transparency is on. Turn it off in **Settings > Appearance > Glass Window**.
- **The Mac app updates itself** from this version on, signed like on Windows and Linux. If you have 0.2.7 on the Mac, download 0.2.8 by hand this one time.

### Cleaner everywhere

- **Messages:**
  - The conversation list has plain rows, one unread badge and a tag on requests.
  - In a conversation, messages are grouped, with one time per group and proper day labels. Removed messages and typing show quietly, and the message field is calm.
  - The launcher asks before you block someone, unsend a message or leave.
- **Friends, Party and calls** look like one family, and the friend pickers show pictures.
- **Game settings, controls and advanced** open as full-screen pages instead of side drawers.
  - Game settings come in three groups.
  - Questions such as uninstall, restore, close game or delete are one centred card, and Cancel is chosen first.
- **Home:**
  - The button says **Back to Game** while a game runs.
  - At most four facts sit under the title, and the update notice appears in one place only.
  - The selected tile shows the game's full name.
  - An empty library tells you what to do.
  - The start screen has a Cancel button for the mouse.
- **Settings** are grouped under captions.
- **Profile and Compare:**
  - Your level shows its progress, and trophies show their grades.
  - A private profile still offers Add Friend.
- **Setup:** every step has a Continue button, and the folder step has **Not now**.
- **Errors** say what to do next, and stay until you close them.
- **Offline**, the launcher only offers what can work.
- **The button hints** sit at the bottom right everywhere and name what the key does right now.
- **Text and shapes:** text, surfaces and corners use fewer, steadier values. Motion is calmer, and initials are readable on light accent colours.
- **Cut-off text:** a name or message that is cut off shows in full when you rest on it.

### Downloads

- Windows: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.8-Setup.exe` (installer, no administrator rights needed) or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.8-x64.exe` (one file).
- Linux: `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.8-x86_64.AppImage` or `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.8-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`.
- macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or newer): `AnyPlay-Launcher-0.2.8-macos-arm64.zip`. Unzip it and move the app to Applications. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- The launcher you have on Windows or Linux offers this update by itself. It is signed, like every update since 0.2.0.

## AnyPlay Launcher 0.2.6

### New

- **Messages work like on a console.** **Friends > Messages** lists your conversations with the last message in each; open one and the thread fills the screen. You can:
  - reply to a message, edit it within 15 minutes, unsend it, copy it or report it;
  - mute or pin a conversation;
  - see when a friend is typing and when your message was delivered and read;
  - answer with a quick reply, or type on the on-screen keyboard with a controller.

  A message from someone who is not your friend arrives as a request, which you accept or block first.
- **Game Music**: rest on a game on the home screen and its own theme music fades in, in place of the home screen music. Move on and it fades back. Turn it off in **Settings > Behavior > Game Music**.
- **The Party page is a room.** Members appear as large pictures that show what they play, who leads and who is speaking. Invite, chat, share your screen, start Play Together and leave from one place, and change voice settings without leaving.
- **Add to Steam** (Windows and Linux): in **Settings > Behavior**, **Add to Steam** puts the AnyPlay Launcher into your Steam library with its artwork, and **Add My Games to Steam** adds each of your games with its own pictures. A game's entry opens the launcher on that game, and you press Play there. Close Steam first: Steam rewrites its list when it quits.
- **Unlocked Games Location**: in **Settings > Library** you can keep unlocked games on another drive. The launcher moves the copies you already have, with your saves, and shows how far it is; you can cancel and continue later. Your game files are never changed.

### Look and feel

- The focus ring shows only while you use the keyboard or a controller, and it is quieter. A click hides it; the next arrow key or stick press brings it back where you were.
